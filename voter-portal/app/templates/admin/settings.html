How it works

  • Hidden voters are not deleted. Imports keep updating them, and they appear automatically if their party changes to one you show (for example, when they join the MDP list).
  • Admins see the campaign view too, and can tap Show all parties in the banner to look someone up. That only changes the view for the admin who taps it, and it's logged.
  • Houses with no voters in the campaign drop off the dashboard and the assignment lists.
